## UNDERSTANDING GEO (GENERATIVE ENGINE OPTIMIZATION)

GEO is the practice of optimizing digital content so that AI-powered search engines — ChatGPT, Google Gemini, Claude, Perplexity, and others — select, synthesize, and cite your brand in their generated responses. Unlike traditional SEO (ranking blue links), GEO focuses on:

1. **Entity Recognition** — Can AI identify your brand as a distinct entity?
2. **Citation Worthiness** — Would AI cite your content as authoritative?
3. **Machine Readability** — Can AI parse and understand your content structure?
4. **Fact Density** — Does your content provide high-value factual information?
5. **Consensus Signals** — Does your brand information appear consistently across the web?

## 2. CRITICAL GEO IMPLEMENTATIONS

### 2.1 Schema Markup (Foundation for AI Discovery)

**This is the single most important factor for AI discoverability.**

AI systems use schema markup to:
- Identify what entities exist on your site (brand, products, organization)
- Understand relationships between entities (product → brand → category)
- Extract factual data (pricing, availability, ratings)
- Build knowledge graph connections

**Required Schema Types (in priority order):**

| Schema Type | Implementation Location | Why AI Needs It |
|:------------|:----------------------|:----------------|
| Organization | Homepage, Contact, Footer | Establishes brand as an entity in AI knowledge graphs |
| Product | Every product page | Enables AI to identify, categorize, and recommend products |
| Offer | Within Product schema | Provides pricing and availability for AI shopping queries |
| ProductGroup | Product pages with variants | Groups sizes/colors so AI understands product variations |
| BreadcrumbList | All pages | Shows AI the site structure and content relationships |
| CollectionPage | Category/collection pages | Helps AI understand product groupings |
| AggregateRating | Product pages (with reviews) | Trust signal for AI recommendations |
| FAQPage | FAQ/help pages | Directly answers AI conversational queries |
